# 50일 커리큘럼 (토익 700+ 기준)

하루 40단어 = 회화(talk) 20 + 금융(fin) 20. 기본 단어(company, profit, meeting 등)는 이미 안다고 보고 뺀다.
1~17일 기초(토익 700~800: 비즈니스 연어, 정확한 동사), 18~34일 중급(실무·면접), 35~50일 고급(뉘앙스, 원어민식 표현).

## 기초 (1~17일)
1. talk: 자기소개를 세련되게(major in, be keen on, background in, transition into) · fin: 손익 핵심(top line, bottom line, COGS, SG&A, gross margin)
2. talk: 일과·바쁨 표현(swamped, juggle, tied up, wind down) · fin: 수익성 지표(operating margin, EBIT, EBITDA, margin expansion/compression)
3. talk: 공부·전공 이야기(coursework, elective, take on, fall behind) · fin: 재무상태표(current asset, fixed asset, retained earnings, book value)
4. talk: 계획·약속(pencil in, reschedule, push back, tentative) · fin: 현금흐름표(operating/investing/financing cash flow, free cash flow, cash burn)
5. talk: 의견 시작 표현(from my perspective, I'd argue, to be fair) · fin: 성장 지표(CAGR, YoY, QoQ, organic growth, run-rate)
6. talk: 맞장구·리액션(fair point, makes sense, I hear you, no way) · fin: 시장 분석(TAM, market share, barriers to entry, consolidation)
7. talk: 되묻기·확인(come again, run that by me, just to clarify) · fin: 비용 구조(fixed/variable cost, operating leverage, overhead, economies of scale)
8. talk: 감정의 뉘앙스(frustrated, relieved, overwhelmed, thrilled) · fin: 주식 기본(market cap, outstanding shares, dividend yield, buyback)
9. talk: 정중한 부탁(would you mind, I was wondering if, could I ask a favor) · fin: 밸류에이션 기초(intrinsic value, multiple, P/E, EV/EBITDA)
10. talk: 일상 구동사 1(figure out, come up with, end up, look into) · fin: 투자 지표(ROE, ROIC, payback period, hurdle rate)
11. talk: 부드러운 반대(I see it differently, not necessarily, I'm not so sure) · fin: 부채 기초(principal, interest expense, maturity, collateral, default)
12. talk: 경험 이야기(back when, at the time, it turned out, looking back) · fin: 예산·계획(guidance, budget variance, forecast, projection, outlook)
13. talk: 돈·소비 표현(splurge, budget-friendly, afford, rip-off) · fin: 투자 기초(risk-adjusted return, diversification, allocation, volatility)
14. talk: 전화·화상 통화(hop on a call, you're breaking up, mute, catch up) · fin: 재무제표 읽기(10-K, annual report, footnote, fiscal year, MD&A)
15. talk: 사과·거절 세련되게(I'm afraid, I'll have to pass, my apologies) · fin: 유동성·안정성(current ratio, quick ratio, solvency, debt-to-equity)
16. talk: 연어 1(make a decision, take a break, raise a concern, meet a deadline) · fin: 금융 업계 구조(sell-side, buy-side, bulge bracket, boutique, coverage)
17. talk: 1~16일 회화 종합 · fin: 1~16일 금융 종합(unit economics, KPI, benchmark)

## 중급 (18~34일)
18. talk: 첫 출근·네트워킹(onboard, get up to speed, reach out, touch base) · fin: M&A 기본(acquirer, target, strategic/financial buyer, deal flow)
19. talk: 업무 진행 공유(on track, behind schedule, bottleneck, heads-up) · fin: 실사(due diligence, data room, red flag, Q&A log, findings)
20. talk: 이메일 시작·마무리(I hope this finds you well, please advise, best regards) · fin: 밸류에이션 방법(DCF, WACC, terminal value, comps, precedent transactions)
21. talk: 회의 진행(kick off, on the agenda, take the floor, wrap up) · fin: PE 구조(LP, GP, commitment, management fee, carried interest)
22. talk: 논리적 의견(the bottom line is, on top of that, that said) · fin: LBO(sponsor, equity check, debt capacity, exit multiple, deleveraging)
23. talk: 반대·이견 조율(push back, play devil's advocate, meet halfway) · fin: 부채 구조(senior, subordinated, mezzanine, unitranche, capital stack)
24. talk: 명확화 요청(could you elaborate, what do you mean by, walk me through) · fin: 거래 효과(accretive, dilutive, synergy, EPS accretion, pro forma)
25. talk: 마감·우선순위(prioritize, tight deadline, bandwidth, slip) · fin: 거래 구조(carve-out, spin-off, divestiture, minority stake, joint venture)
26. talk: 직장 구동사(follow up, sign off on, iron out, loop in) · fin: 대출 계약(covenant, tranche, amortization schedule, bullet repayment)
27. talk: 문제 보고·해결 제안(flag an issue, workaround, root cause) · fin: 신디케이션·리파이낸싱(arranger, syndication, refinancing, spread, pricing grid)
28. talk: 피드백(constructive, on point, room for improvement, take it on board) · fin: 회계 1(accrual, depreciation, amortization, impairment, write-off)
29. talk: 협업·팀워크(align, pull one's weight, step up, cover for) · fin: 운전자본(working capital, DSO, DPO, inventory turnover, cash conversion cycle)
30. talk: 숫자·추세 설명(plateau, spike, bottom out, outperform) · fin: 감사·FAS(audit, assurance, advisory, QoE, valuation report)
31. talk: 면접 1: 자기소개, 지원 동기(drawn to, aligns with, passionate about) · fin: 재무 모델링(assumption, driver, sensitivity, scenario, circular reference)
32. talk: 면접 2: 경험 말하기(STAR, spearheaded, took ownership, outcome) · fin: 회계 2(goodwill, purchase price allocation, provision, contingent liability)
33. talk: 협상 표현(leverage, counteroffer, deal-breaker, common ground) · fin: 딜 단계(NDA, LOI, term sheet, SPA, signing, closing)
34. talk: 18~33일 직장 회화 종합 · fin: 18~33일 금융 종합(net debt, enterprise value, equity bridge)

## 고급 (35~50일)
35. talk: 프레젠테이션 구조(walk you through, to recap, moving on to) · fin: 자본시장 개요(ECM, DCM, primary/secondary market, issuer)
36. talk: 설득·근거(compelling, substantiate, the case for, make a strong case) · fin: 인수(underwriting, underwriter, firm commitment, best efforts, gross spread)
37. talk: 고급 이메일(gentle reminder, circle back, per my last email) · fin: IPO 1(prospectus, S-1, listing, roadshow, anchor investor)
38. talk: 미팅 정리(action items, takeaways, owner, next steps) · fin: IPO 2(bookbuilding, price range, oversubscribed, allocation, greenshoe)
39. talk: 유보·조건부 동의(in principle, with reservations, provided that) · fin: 채권(coupon, yield to maturity, credit rating, investment grade, high yield)
40. talk: 클라이언트 응대(manage expectations, deliverable, turnaround time) · fin: 주식 후속 발행(follow-on, block trade, lock-up, rights issue, convertible)
41. talk: 에스컬레이션·위기 대응(escalate, damage control, contingency plan) · fin: 신용·구조조정(distressed, restructuring, haircut, debt-for-equity swap)
42. talk: 커피챗·네트워킹 고급(pick your brain, keep in touch, put in a good word) · fin: IC와 투자 메모(investment thesis, upside case, downside protection, key risks)
43. talk: 면접 3: 시장·딜 의견(I'd be cautious about, the key driver is) · fin: 시장 지표(basis point, spread widening, risk-off, flight to quality)
44. talk: 면접 4: 압박 질문 대응(that's a great question, let me think about that) · fin: 거시·금리(rate hike, tightening, yield curve, inversion, inflation expectations)
45. talk: 비즈니스 관용구(ballpark, move the needle, low-hanging fruit, back to square one) · fin: 이메일 금융 표현(per our discussion, please find attached, for your review, redline)
46. talk: 교환학생 토론·팀플(build on that, chime in, I'd like to add) · fin: 미팅 금융 표현(walk through the numbers, sanity check, deep dive, key takeaway)
47. talk: 불만 제기·협상 마무리(raise a concern, compromise, seal the deal) · fin: 포트폴리오 관리(value creation, add-on, bolt-on, operational improvement, 100-day plan)
48. talk: 시사·문화 스몰토크(trending, controversial, take on, weigh in) · fin: 엑싯과 수익률(IRR, MOIC, secondary buyout, trade sale, dividend recap)
49. talk: 면접 5: 마무리 질문·감사(follow-up, appreciate your time, next steps) · fin: 규제·공시(disclosure, compliance, filing, regulator, insider trading)
50. talk: 전체 회화 종합 · fin: 전체 금융 종합(실무 약어와 연어)
